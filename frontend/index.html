The Early Drift Forecaster intercepted all 5 latent defects with 124-147 hours of advance warning, but flagged the single healthy survivor — a trade-off inherent to symmetric unsupervised screening on a cohort with N=6 and only 1 survivor."

Important Limitation: With only 6 physical components and 1 healthy survivor, a single false positive mathematically yields FPR=100%. McNemar exact tests cannot reach p<0.05 at this sample size. Directional (one-sided) screening is recommended for future production deployment to eliminate the symmetric distance artifact.

Audited Scientific Findings on Predictive ESS Screening

"On this dataset, predictive AI/ML and dynamic statistical screening demonstrated substantially improved latent defect recall (60% to 100% vs 0% for traditional screening) with 139 to 142 hours of predictive lead time, but at the cost of increased false alarms / yield loss on this small-sample cohort (N=6)."

Demonstrated Scientific Contributions

  • Predictive Kinetic Advantage: Trajectory forecasting achieves 100% recall vs 0% for static limits.
  • Advance Warning Notice: Provides 124h to 147h lead time before specification breach.
  • Transparent Explainability: Every rejection explained via standardized physical deviations.
  • Ablation Confirmation: Trajectory forecasting proved superior to purely spatial anomaly clustering.

Small-Sample Boundaries & Limitations

  • Single Survivor Penalty: 1 false positive on C1 mathematically yields 100% nominal FPR.
  • Sample Size Bound (N=6): Exact McNemar tests yield p=0.22-0.38; frequentist power limited.
  • Symmetric Distance Flaw: Unsupervised 2-sided tests penalize low degradation rates.
  • Future Scope: Directional screening and larger multi-lot validation cohorts required.
